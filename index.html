About Me

I am currently a Ph.D. student at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University under the supervision of Prof. Hongxia Yang. Previously, I obtained my MSc from Dalian University of Technology under the supervision of Prof. Huchuan Lu. My research focuses on Large Language Models, Multimodal GUI Agents, and Diffusion Models for Video Generation.
